How to pick a giveaway winner

  1. Paste all the entries — one name per line — in the box.
  2. Click Update wheel. Every name gets its own slice of the wheel.
  3. Press SPIN THE WHEEL. The wheel spins and stops on one random winner.
  4. Need 2nd and 3rd place too? Click Remove winner & spin again and spin for the next one.
  5. Want to share this exact wheel? Click Copy share link and send it to anyone.

Questions

Is the winner really random?

Yes. Every spin picks a random entry — every name on the wheel has exactly the same chance of winning.

How many entries can I add?

Up to 100 names. Paste them one per line, or separated by commas.

Can I pick more than one winner?

Yes. After a win, click "Remove winner & spin again" — the winner leaves the wheel and you spin for the next place.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no payment, nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your names stay in your own browser.

Can I use this live on a stream?

Yes — open the wheel full screen in your browser and share your screen. Everyone watching sees the spin happen live.
