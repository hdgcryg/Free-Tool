5. Your country — voltage & sun

US and Canadian outlets run on 120 V; UK outlets run on 230 V. A power station bought in one country usually cannot just be plugged into the other's wall sockets — check the outlets and the plug type before you buy.

How the calculator works

  1. Usable energy = battery Wh × usable %. A 1,000 Wh station at 85% gives you 850 Wh to actually use.
  2. Draw from the battery = your load ÷ inverter efficiency + idle draw. The inverter loses about 12% turning battery power into wall power.
  3. Runtime = usable energy ÷ draw. That is the whole formula — the same one EcoFlow, Jackery and Bluetti guides use.
  4. Fridges and freezers use their average draw for runtime (they cycle on and off — a full-size fridge averages about 60 W even though the compressor draws 150 W while running), but the output and surge checks use the full running and starting watts.
  5. Start-up peak = all running watts together + the single biggest starting surge. If that peak is above the station's surge rating, the station may shut off when a motor starts — start big appliances one at a time.
  6. Solar charging = battery Wh ÷ (panel watts × 75%). A 200 W panel gives about 150 W of real charging power in good sun.

Common questions

How long will a 1,000 Wh power station run a refrigerator?

About 11 hours on the fridge alone — a full-size fridge averages around 60 W, so 850 usable Wh ÷ (60 ÷ 0.88 + 10 idle) ≈ 11 hours. Add a TV and lights and it drops to roughly 3–4 hours.

Why is the runtime shorter than Wh ÷ watts?

Three things eat into it: you cannot use 100% of the battery, the inverter loses ~12% as heat, and the inverter itself burns 5–20 W just staying on. That is why a 1,000 Wh station gives about 850 Wh of usable energy, not 1,000.

Can a power station run a microwave or kettle?

Only if its continuous output is high enough — a microwave draws about 1,500 W and a kettle the same. Most 1,000 Wh stations (1,500 W output) can run one of them, but not both at once, and only for a short time: 850 Wh ÷ 1,500 W is about half an hour.

What does surge power mean?

Motors and compressors need a short burst of extra power to start — a fridge needs about 600 W for a few seconds even though it runs on 150 W. If the burst is bigger than the station's surge rating, the station trips. That is the number people forget to check.

How long does solar take to recharge it?

A 1,000 Wh station with a 200 W panel needs about 6.7 hours of full sun (1,000 ÷ 150). At 4 peak sun hours a day that is about 1.7 days. Bigger panels or two panels in parallel cut the time.
