How it works

  1. Enter your date of birth (and time, if you know it).
  2. Pick the age you expect to live until — 80 is the default.
  3. Watch your lived time tick up live, second by second, and your remaining time tick down.
  4. Copy your result and share it with a friend — who has lived longer, you or them?

Questions

Is the "time left" number real?

No — it is a countdown to the age you typed in, nothing more. Average life expectancy in most countries is around 70–80 years, but that is an average across millions of people. It cannot predict any one person's life. This tool is for fun and reflection, not a prediction.

Why do the seconds keep running?

Because you are alive right now — every second that passes adds one more second to your life. The numbers update every second, live in your browser.

Is my birthday sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your own browser. Your birthday is never uploaded, stored or shared.

What are the heartbeat and breath numbers?

Rough estimates: an average resting heart beats about 70 times a minute and a person breathes about 16 times a minute. Multiply those by the seconds you have lived and you get a fun sense of the scale — they are estimates, not measurements.
