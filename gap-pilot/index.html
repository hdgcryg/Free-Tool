    How GapPilot packs your plan

    1. It respects your energy. Low energy? Only low-energy tasks get in. High-energy jobs wait for a better moment.
    2. It respects where you are. A task that needs the office won't be packed while you're at home.
    3. It respects order. Tasks that depend on another task only go in after that task. And your list order is your priority — top first.
    4. It adds real costs. Every task gets 1 minute to start and 1 minute to switch, because in real life nothing starts instantly.
    5. It keeps a buffer. 10% of your time stays free, so one slow task doesn't wreck the whole plan.
    6. It fills honestly. If a task doesn't fit, it says so — and tells you why. It never invents filler tasks.

    Questions

    Why did my plan leave free time?

    Because the next task didn't fit in the time left — and packing a task you can't finish is worse than leaving the minutes free. Use the Shuffle button to try a different combination, or pick a shorter task.

    What does "needs high energy" mean?

    Each task has an energy level you set. If you tell GapPilot your energy is low, it only packs low-energy tasks, so the plan is one you can actually do right now.

    Why is there a safety buffer?

    Because real life runs late. The buffer is 10% of your available time (at least 2 minutes) kept free on purpose — it's what makes the plan realistic instead of a fantasy.

    Is my task list saved anywhere?

    No. Everything happens in your browser. Your tasks are never uploaded or stored.
