    Where the priorities come from

    These are not invented. They come from published analysis of Punjab Board papers:

    • Very High — Chapters 2, 3, 5, 7: Kinematics, Dynamics, Gravitation and Properties of Matter produce the majority of Section B and all Section C questions in 9th Physics past papers from 2018–2025, across all Punjab boards — and have the highest numerical repetition.
    • High — Chapter 6: Work and Energy numericals are high-frequency in the objective and long-question sections.
    • High — Chapter 9: Transfer of Heat carries 2 MCQs and 3 short questions in the 2026 pairing scheme — the most short questions of any chapter — and stands alone for long questions.
    • Medium — Chapters 1, 4, 8: still in the paper (every chapter contributes MCQs), but with less weight.

    Sources: 9th Class Physics past papers analysis (2018–2025) · 9th Class Pairing Scheme 2026 (PECTA). Paper pattern: 60 marks — 12 MCQs, 30 short questions, 18 long questions. Weightage varies by board and session, so use this as a guide, not a guarantee.

    Questions

    Why is Chapter 2 first if I haven't finished Chapter 1?

    Because the paper rewards it more. Kinematics appears in short questions, long questions and numericals — finishing it first earns more marks per hour than finishing Chapter 1 first. Chapter 1 is still on the list, just later.

    What if I have very few days left?

    The tool will tell you honestly. If the pace is impossible, study only the Very High chapters first (2, 3, 5, 7) — they carry most of the paper — then add Chapter 6 and 9 if time allows.

    Does this work for other boards or classes?

    Not yet — this page is built only for Punjab Board 9th Physics, because that is where the chapter weights are published and checked. Other subjects and classes are coming.

    Is my progress saved anywhere?

    No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
